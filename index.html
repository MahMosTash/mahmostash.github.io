There are people behind the data.

A metric going up isn't enough. I want to understand the decisions behind it. These four questions connect most of what I do.

  1. Q1

    When a system makes decisions, how do people respond?

    An incentive can get a driver to do more rides today. Does it help them, or just buy short-term behavior? I care what happens after the metric moves.

    → incentive work at Snapp → play the bandit

  2. Q2

    Can AI help people decide better, not just predict better?

    AI is judged by accuracy. The real test is whether people make better calls with it. Next for Driver Voice: from flagging problems to helping teams decide what to fix.

    → explainable AI in finance → Driver Voice, layer 2

  3. Q3

    Can a model learn markets from markets that never existed?

    Financial data is short, noisy, and keeps shifting. If we pretrain only on simulated markets, which synthetic worlds teach something real?

    → synthetic pre-training → generate a fake market

  4. Q4

    What do economic shocks do to the choices families make?

    Inflation shows up in prices, and in whether a child stays in school. I trace macro shocks down to individual choices.

    → inflation & school attendance
